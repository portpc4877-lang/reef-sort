How to play

Tap a sea creature in a clear bubble. It swims to the bowl asking for it, or waits in the tray.

Three of a kind fill a bowl. Dimmed bubbles are buried, so clear the ones on top first. Ice bubbles thaw after the number of other bubbles pops, and chained bubbles open when you pop the bubble with the key.

Don't let all 6 tray slots fill up! Use Net, Clear and Shuffle when stuck, and tap + to open a 4th bowl.
