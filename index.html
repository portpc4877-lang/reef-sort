How to play

Tap a sea creature in a clear bubble. It swims to the bowl asking for it, or waits in the tray.

Three of a kind fill a bowl. Dimmed bubbles are covered, so clear the ones on top first.

Don't let all 6 tray slots fill up! Use Net, Clear and Shuffle when stuck, and tap + to open a 4th bowl.
